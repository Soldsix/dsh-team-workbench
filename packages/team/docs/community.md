# Community participation / 社区参与规范

Discuss technical work respectfully. Explain disagreements with reproducible evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, and give contributors room to correct mistakes. Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

请尊重参与者，用可复现证据讨论分歧，明确不确定之处，允许他人纠正错误。不得骚扰、歧视、威胁、冒充他人或公开他人的隐私。

Use synthetic examples in public issues and reviews. Do not post credentials, authenticated URLs, complete Sessions, persona bodies or identifiable device data. Report software vulnerabilities through the process in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

公开讨论使用合成示例，不上传密钥、认证 URL、完整会话、人设正文或可识别设备的数据。软件漏洞按[安全说明](../SECURITY.md)报告。

Maintainers may hide abusive content, request edits, restrict participation or remove access according to the severity and repetition of the conduct. Use GitHub's private reporting tools for harassment or privacy concerns; do not repost the sensitive material in an issue.

维护者可按影响和重复情况隐藏违规内容、要求修改、限制参与或取消访问。骚扰和隐私问题请使用 GitHub 的私密举报工具，不要在 Issue 中再次公开相关敏感材料。
